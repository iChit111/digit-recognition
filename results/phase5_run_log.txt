======================================================================
  CONCLUSION
======================================================================
  Top models : SVM (RBF) (80.90%) and Dense Neural Network (80.36%) are statistically tied (0.5 pp, p = 0.071).
               Dense Neural Network has the higher macro F1 (65.46%), i.e. it handles rare classes better.
  Weakest    : Logistic Regression (71.56%) — a linear model cannot
               capture curved stroke shapes even with interaction features.
  Digits are recognised far better than letters in every model. The errors come
  from characters that are drawn the same way: l / I -> 1, O <-> 0, S <-> 5, and
  case-only pairs (m/M, c/C, u/U...: 20% of SVM (RBF)'s errors). Lowercase
  look-alikes are almost never predicted, because their uppercase twin is more
  common in the training data — the class imbalance found in Phases 1 and 3.
